Colour Ramp & Theme Generator

Compare RGB mixing with OKLCH, inspect the calculations, test text contrast, and export reusable CSS tokens for your project.

1 · Choose a starting colour and method

2 · Generated 11-step colour ramp

Step 500 is always your exact input colour. Steps 50–400 are lighter; 600–950 are darker. Swatches show generated HEX values and contrast against white and black.

3 · Understand the numbers

4 · CSS foundation tokens

Copy these variables into tokens.css. The semantic tokens below the ramp provide starter light and dark themes.



5 · Built-in WCAG text contrast checker
WCAG 2.x AA: normal text ≥ 4.5:1; large text ≥ 3:1. This checks text contrast, not all other accessibility requirements.


6 · Application theme previews
These previews use semantic tokens generated from your ramp. They are independent of the tool appearance selector above. Check each text/background combination before using the palette in a real application.


7 · Student investigation
Reproduce the NSCC case study using #004780. Compare the 400 and 600 steps under both methods. Then enter your own project's starting colour, generate both ramps, record differences, and test your light and dark theme contrast.
Important: RGB mixing interpolates sRGB channels toward white or black. OKLCH mode adjusts perceptual lightness and chroma, converts back to sRGB, and reduces chroma when needed to fit the display gamut. Neither method guarantees accessibility; always check contrast.
