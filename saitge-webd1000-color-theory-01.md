## 🎨 Understanding Hue, Shade, Tint, and Tone

### What is a Hue?
A **hue** is a pure color — like red, blue, yellow, or green. It’s the starting point for creating other variations of color.

---

### Modifying a Hue

| Term   | What’s Added | What Happens | Example |
|--------|--------------|--------------|---------|
| **Shade** | Black        | Darkens the hue | Red → Maroon |
| **Tint**  | White        | Lightens the hue | Red → Pink |
| **Tone**  | Grey (black + white) | Softens the hue | Red → Dusty Rose |

---

### Visual Concept

- **Shade** = Hue + Black → deeper, richer color
- **Tint** = Hue + White → lighter, pastel-like color
- **Tone** = Hue + Grey → muted, less intense color

---

### Why It Matters
Understanding these variations helps in:
- **Art & Design**: Creating mood and contrast
- **Digital Media**: Choosing accessible and appealing color palettes
- **Everyday Life**: Picking colors for clothes, interiors, and presentations
