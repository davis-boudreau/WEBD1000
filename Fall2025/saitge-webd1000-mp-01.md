# Mini-Project 1 (MP1): Site Evaluation & Planning

**Weight:** 10% of Final Grade
**Due:** End of Week 3
**Format:** PDF or Word document (2–3 pages, plus diagrams/tables)

---

## Project Overview

For this mini-project, you will **choose an idea for a simple website** that you will continue developing throughout the course. By the end of the term, this site will include at least:

* A **header** (site name + navigation)
* A **hero section** (introductory content, call-to-action, or main highlight)
* A **footer** (basic contact details, copyright, or quick links)

MP1 is about **planning your site**. You will start by **evaluating an existing website**, then propose a redesigned version with a clear **content inventory** and **sitemap**.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Choose Your Website Idea

Pick a site concept that is relevant to you. Examples:

* Personal **portfolio**
* Local **club or community** site
* **Small business** services site
* **Event** or **organization** homepage

👉 Write 2–3 sentences explaining your idea, audience, and purpose.

---

### 2. Evaluate an Existing Website (Usability + Accessibility)

Find a site similar to the one you want to create. Review it for:

* **Usability:** clear navigation, labeling, clutter vs. clarity
* **Accessibility:** keyboard navigation, text readability, contrast, alt text, mobile responsiveness

👉 Record strengths (what works) and weaknesses (what should improve).

---

### 3. Create a Content Inventory

List the **pages and content items** your site will include. Use a table like this:

| Page/Section | Content Item | Type (Text, Image, Video, Form) | Source (Existing/New) | Priority (High/Med/Low) | Notes             |
| ------------ | ------------ | ------------------------------- | --------------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------- |
| Homepage     | Welcome text | Text                            | Write new             | High                    | Keep short        |
| Homepage     | Hero image   | Image                           | Personal photo        | High                    | Optimize for web  |
| About        | Short bio    | Text                            | Write new             | High                    | Plain language    |
| Contact      | Contact form | Form                            | New                   | High                    | Accessible labels |

---

### 4. Draw a Sitemap

Sketch or create a simple sitemap showing how pages connect.

* Limit top navigation to **5–7 items**.
* Group related pages.
* Keep labels consistent.

👉 You can draw on paper and scan/take a photo, or use a tool like **draw\.io**, **Lucidchart**, or **Miro**.

---

### 5. Write Recommendations & Reflection

Write 1–2 paragraphs addressing:

* How your sitemap improves usability compared to the evaluated site.
* What accessibility considerations you built into your structure.
* Which content is most important for your audience.
* How this planning stage sets you up for building a **header, hero, and footer** in future milestones.

---

## Deliverables

Your submission must include:

1. **Website idea statement** (2–3 sentences).
2. **Evaluation of an existing site** (strengths + weaknesses).
3. **Content inventory table** (at least 3–5 pages).
4. **Sitemap diagram**.
5. **Recommendations & reflection** (150–200 words).
