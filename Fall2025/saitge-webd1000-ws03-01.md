# Week 3 Workshop & MP1 Guide

**Topic:** Requirements & Planning – Site Maps & Content Hierarchy
**Assessment:** MP1 (10%) – Site Evaluation & Recommendations

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

* Apply usability heuristics and accessibility checks to evaluate an existing site.
* Create a content inventory to capture what information a site needs.
* Develop a sitemap showing a logical, accessible content structure.
* Write recommendations for redesign based on your findings.

## Step 2: Evaluate the Existing Site (30 min)

1. **Usability Heuristics Check (Nielsen’s simplified set):**

   * Is the navigation clear and consistent?
   * Can you tell where you are at all times?
   * Is the layout cluttered or clean?
   * Are links/buttons labeled clearly?

2. **Accessibility Quick Check:**

   * Can you tab through the site with only a keyboard?
   * Is text readable (contrast, font size)?
   * Do images have alt text (inspect one or two)?
   * Does the site work on mobile view (use browser dev tools)?

## Step 4: Create a Sitemap (40 min)

1. **Take your inventory → group items into pages.**
2. **Sketch a sitemap:** boxes = pages, arrows = navigation links.

   * Example structure:

     ```
     Home
      ├── About
      ├── Services
      │     ├── Service 1
      │     ├── Service 2
      └── Contact
     ```
3. Use pen/paper, or tools like **draw\.io**, **Miro**, or **Lucidchart**.
4. Keep top navigation simple (max 5–7 items).

## Step 5: Write Recommendations & Reflection (20–30 min)

Your report should have 3 parts:

1. **Site Evaluation Summary (1 page)**

   * Strengths (what works well).
   * Weaknesses (usability/accessibility issues).

2. **Proposed Redesign Plan (1 page)**

   * Content inventory table.
   * Sitemap diagram.
   * Short explanation of choices.

3. **Reflection (½ page)**

   * How did your audience shape your sitemap?
   * Which usability or accessibility problems were most important to fix?
   * What will you focus on in the next milestone?
